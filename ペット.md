## Web prototype / Web試作品

Open `ペット.html` in a browser. No install needed. Data is saved in the browser (localStorage).
`ペット.html` をブラウザで開くだけで使えます。データはブラウザ内に保存されます。

Works in the prototype: pet profiles with photo, multiple pets, 1-tap care records, daily checklist, calendar, history, English/Japanese, dark mode, text sharing.

Not in the prototype (planned for Android): local notifications, QR code, AdMob ads, Room database.

## Planned tech (Android)

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room, DataStore, AdMob (test ads during development).
No API keys or signing files in this repository.

## Prototype fixes / 試作品の修正（2026-11-15）

- Show a persistent English/Japanese warning when browser storage fails; clear it after a successful save.
  ブラウザへの保存に失敗した場合は警告を表示し、保存成功後に解除します。
- Include amounts and walk duration in shared care logs.
  共有するお世話記録に量・散歩の分数を含めます。
- Move the selected date along with the calendar month, handling short months and leap years.
  月移動時に選択日も移動し、月末やうるう年を考慮します。
- Refresh the display after a date change (checked every 30 seconds and when returning to the page). While a form is open, wait until it is closed to preserve input.
  日付の変更を30秒ごと・画面復帰時に確認します。入力フォームが開いている間は更新を待ち、入力を保ちます。
- Require at least one repeat weekday instead of silently changing an empty selection to every day.
  繰り返す曜日の未選択を案内し、勝手に毎日に変更しません。
